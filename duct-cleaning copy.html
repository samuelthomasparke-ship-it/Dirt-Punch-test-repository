FAQ

Common Questions

How long does duct cleaning take? +

Most single-family homes take 2–3 hours for a full-system clean, depending on vent count and system size.

How often should I get my ducts cleaned? +

We recommend every 2–3 years for most homes, or annually if you have pets, allergies, or recent renovation dust.

Do you offer sanitizing treatments? +

Yes — our Deep Clean and Whole Home packages include an EPA-registered sanitizing treatment to address odors and mold spores.
